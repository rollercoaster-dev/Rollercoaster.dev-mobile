Plan in the list, not in a modal

Dating a plan is one pass down the whole list, and every date is decided against the steps around it. One affordance per step instead of two ghost chips, timing and sequence in one place, and any day reachable rather than three canned ones.

What changed, and why

One way in, not two

Every row and sub-row used to carry + depends and + date. On this goal that's sixteen dashed placeholders reading as unfilled fields — the opposite of "setting must never feel required." Now a step shows its timing, or one quiet ＋ when?

The plan stays on screen

A bottom sheet scrims the list at the exact moment you need it. "Panels the week after the inspection" is unanswerable when the inspection is behind the scrim. The row expands in place instead, and its neighbours stay visible.

Your other steps are on the calendar

The small badges mark days where other steps already sit. You place this one against the plan rather than from memory — the part no picker widget could fix.

Any day, including the awkward ones

Months navigate and past days stay selectable, so there is no "none of these three is the day I want" — and no day the editing case can't reopen.

Timing and sequence, one decision

"Panels come after the inspection, so around the 30th" is a single thought. Two chips opening two sheets split it in half and then let the halves contradict each other silently.

"Depends on", not "comes after"

Step 4 comes after step 3 by being step 4 — that label restated the list order instead of naming a dependency. Not GitHub's blocked by, though: complete stays live on a step whose dependency is still open, so nothing here is actually blocked.

It informs, it never enforces

Put a step before the one it follows and you get a plain sentence stating where the other step sits. No red, no warning icon, nothing disabled, nothing refused.

"Waiting on" isn't here

An external wait is a fact about the world you record mid-ride, when it starts to bite — not while laying out a plan. It belongs in Focus, on the step you're actually standing on, and shares nothing with this surface but its read-out.
